Distributed Systems Basics

Introduction: What Is Raft?

Raft is a consensus algorithm that allows multiple servers to agree on a consistent sequence of operations, even when individual servers fail.

Consensus

Multiple nodes agree on the same committed log history.

Leader-Based

One elected leader coordinates log replication across all cluster nodes.

Fault Tolerance

The cluster continues operating seamlessly despite certain node failures.

Consistency

Committed operations remain identical and consistent across the cluster.

Why Does This Matter?

Distributed systems need to prevent conflicting writes, inconsistent state, and data loss during failures.

How Raft Chooses a Leader

Understanding node state transitions and the 5-step consensus voting mechanism.

Follower

Each node starts as a follower and receives heartbeats and replication requests from the leader. Each follower also has an election timeout.

Candidate

A follower becomes a candidate when its election timeout expires without receiving valid leader communication.

Leader

Once a follower becomes a candidate it requests votes from follower nodes. Once it has a majority of votes the candidate becomes a Leader. Which a leader coordinates writes and replicates log entries to followers.

The Election Process
01

Election Timeout Expires

A follower's election timeout expires.

02

Becomes a Candidate

It becomes a candidate and increments its term while it also votes for itself.

03

Requests Votes

The candidate then requests votes from other nodes.

04

Wins Majority

If it receives a majority of votes, it becomes leader.

05

Sends Heartbeats

The new leader sends heartbeats to maintain leadership.
