LOG REPLICATION

How Raft Keeps Data Consistent

Raft uses logs to record and replicate storage operations across nodes, ensuring they agree on the same committed operations in the same order. Think of the Raft log as a numbered journal that records storage operations in order. The cluster below has five Vault nodes, with S4 as the leader.
