RAFT INTEGRATED STORAGE

How HashiCorp Vault Uses Raft

Vault uses Raft to keep encrypted data consistent across multiple servers. Integrated Storage lets Vault servers store and replicate encrypted data without a separate database or Consul cluster. The cluster below: 5 nodes - 1 active leader, 4 standby followers, quorum of 3.
